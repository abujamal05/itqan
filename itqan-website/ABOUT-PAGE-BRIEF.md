# Brief: mission, vision, and the "Who we are" page

This is the content and the intent. **How it looks is yours to decide** — use `impeccable` for the
visual direction and the Itqan skills for the values. Nothing below is a layout spec.

Two things to know before you start:

1. **The vision is new.** It was a deliberate TBD in `itqan-brand` §10. It is authored here with the
   lead's sign off and needs writing back into that skill afterwards.
2. **The positioning is the new one, and the skills now agree.** `itqan-brand` §2 used to call Itqan "a
   translation engine"; it has been corrected to the career navigator framing. **The product is the gap,
   the path, and the fit.** Reading the documents is how the measuring starts, not what is being sold.

---

## 1. The shape of the page: Why, then How, then What

Structured on Sinek's golden circle, deliberately. Most about pages open with what the company does,
which is the least persuasive thing they have. This one opens with the belief, because the belief is
the reason a sceptical graduate keeps reading.

**Order: Why → How → What.** Do not reorder it into a feature list.

## 2. It speaks to the graduate, in second person

Not "a graduate finishes their degree". **You.** They are reading this after being rejected without a
reason, so the page talks to them directly and does not describe them from the outside.

> **Arabic note.** Arabic has no gender neutral second person singular, and the anchor user is Maryam.
> The drafts below use the conventional generic masculine, but several lines can be rewritten to avoid
> direct address entirely, which is usually the better answer. **Flag this to the native reviewer as a
> real decision rather than shipping it unexamined.**

---

## 3. Mission and vision

**Mission, internal.** Unchanged, stays as `itqan-brand` §1, not public copy:

> Build the most trusted AI powered career development platform, optimised for the Arabic language and
> the realities of the Arab world, while remaining globally competitive.

**Mission, public.** The internal line says "AI powered", which the voice rules ban as a selling point,
and it describes the company rather than the person reading it:

| | |
|---|---|
| **EN** | We show you how far you are from the job you want, the shortest way to close that distance, and the real jobs that fit you today. |
| **AR** | نُظهر لك كم تبعد عن الوظيفة التي تريدها، وأقصر طريق لإغلاق هذه المسافة، والوظائف الحقيقية التي تناسبك اليوم. |

**Vision.** Recommended, because it ties to the slogan already approved on the deck:

| | |
|---|---|
| **EN** | A Gulf where no graduate has to guess their next step. |
| **AR** | خليجٌ لا يحتاج فيه خريج أن يخمّن خطوته التالية. |

Alternates, if that reads too small:

- *"A Gulf where every graduate knows the distance to the job they want, and the way to close it."*
- *"A labour market where the way from a degree to a job is visible before the effort is spent."*

Pick one. Do not ship two.

## 5. The LinkedIn icon

**Do not draw it.** This site has no icon library today (icons are inline SVG per component), and
Lucide dropped brand icons, so it has no LinkedIn mark.

Use **Simple Icons** — the standard MIT licensed brand icon set, which has an accurate LinkedIn mark.
Any of these is fine, pick whichever fits the build:

- `npm i -D simple-icons` and inline the path at build time. Zero runtime cost, which suits a site with
  a ~1KB JS budget.
- `astro-icon` with `@iconify-json/simple-icons`, if you would rather have the integration.

Make it a real link with an accessible name (`aria-label` naming the person, e.g. "Ahmad Faraj on
LinkedIn"), sized to the 44px touch target minimum, `target="_blank"` with `rel="noopener"`.

The icon is LinkedIn's trademark, so use their actual mark rather than an approximation and do not
recolour it into Itqan's palette on a light surface. Brand blue or a neutral monochrome are both fine.

## 7. Naming and routes

- Arabic `من نحن`. English **"Who we are"** or "About", not "Who are we", which is a question.
- `/ar/about/` and `/en/about/`, English slug under both locales as every other page here does.

## 8. The few hard rules

Everything else is open. These are not:

- **No hyphens or em dashes in prose**, either language. CSS and file identifiers keep theirs.
- **No hype vocabulary**, and never lead with the technology. "AI powered" is banned as a selling point.
- **No invented statistics.** The survey figures are the only numbers approved, and only with their
  qualifier. No accuracy figure has been measured, so none may appear.
- **Do not claim Arabic transcript or CV reading yet.** Arabic first is correct; that capability is
  still being hardened.
- **Tokens only**, no raw values. Brand gold is `#F39F1C`. This site's `tokens.css` still carries the
  retired `#D08C2F`, so use semantic tokens and inherit whatever is current; migrating is a separate job.
- **Bilingual parity is enforced in CI.** Every string in both dictionaries under a new `about` block.
- **RTL is the base.** Logical properties, Arabic body at weight 400, never letter spaced.
- **Every Arabic string needs a native reader's pass**, especially anything containing إتقان.

Hud is allowed here and welcome. Where he appears, use the animated clip, not the still.

Gate before shipping: `python scripts/audit.py src/`, then look at it in both themes, both directions,
narrow and wide.
